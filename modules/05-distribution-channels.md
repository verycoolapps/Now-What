# MODULE 05 — DISTRIBUTION CHANNEL DISCOVERY
## Where to Sell Is as Important as What You Sell

> **Purpose:** "Sell it online" is not a strategy. This module finds the specific, concrete platforms, networks, communities, and channels where your exact target customer actively searches for and buys solutions like yours. The difference between a $0 product and a $1M product is often just distribution.

---

## 5.1 CHANNEL STRATEGY FOUNDATION

### The Distribution Principle
> "The best product distributed through the wrong channel loses to a mediocre product distributed through the right one."

### Channel Fit Criteria
Before evaluating any channel, verify it passes all three tests:

1. **Presence Test:** Are your ICP customers actually on this platform/channel?
2. **Intent Test:** Are they on this platform in a *buying* mindset?
3. **Economics Test:** Does the customer acquisition cost work at this channel's pricing/commission?

Fail any test → deprioritize the channel.

## 5.4 PAID ACQUISITION CHANNELS

**Rule:** Only run paid acquisition when you have verified unit economics. Know your CAC target before spending.

### CAC Target Formula
```
Max CAC = LTV × (1 - desired margin %)
Example: LTV = $500, desired 50% margin → Max CAC = $250
```

| Channel | Avg CPC/CPM | Volume | Best Targeting | Min Budget to Test |

## 5.6 CHANNEL LAUNCH SEQUENCE

### The Minimum Viable Distribution Plan

For a resource-constrained launch (1 person, limited budget):

```
WEEK 1–2: OWNED CHANNELS
→ Build landing page with clear value proposition
→ Set up payment infrastructure (Stripe, Gumroad, or relevant platform)
→ Create basic product listing on the highest-priority marketplace
→ Enable referral mechanism

WEEK 3–4: COMMUNITY CHANNELS
→ Identify 3–5 communities where target customers spend time
→ Establish presence by contributing genuine value
→ Soft-launch through community (no hard sell)

MONTH 2: CONTENT CHANNELS
→ Launch one content format (blog, YouTube, or LinkedIn)
→ Publish minimum 4 pieces of content targeting specific search intent
→ Begin email list building through content

MONTH 3: PAID CHANNELS (if economics justify)
→ Launch small paid test ($300–500) on highest-probability channel
→ Measure CAC vs. LTV
→ Scale what works; cut what doesn't
```

## 5.7 PARTNERSHIP CHANNELS

### Partnership Types for Distribution

| Partnership Type | Description | Revenue Impact | Effort |
|----------------|-------------|---------------|--------|
| **Referral partners** | Others refer customers for commission | Medium | Low |
| **Integration partners** | Integrate with complementary tools | High | Medium |
| **Distribution partners** | Partners distribute your product to their customers | Very High | High |
| **Co-marketing partners** | Joint marketing to each other's audiences | Medium | Medium |
| **Reseller partners** | Partners sell your product independently | High | High |
| **Strategic partners** | Shared go-to-market with aligned companies | Very High | Very High |

### Finding the Right Partners

Target partners who:
1. Serve your exact ICP but do not compete with you
2. Have an established distribution channel you do not have
3. Benefit from your product solving a gap in their offering
4. Have credibility with your target market
