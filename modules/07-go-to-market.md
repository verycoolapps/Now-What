# MODULE 07 — GO-TO-MARKET STRATEGY
## Launch Like a Champion. Not Like an Amateur.

> **Purpose:** A go-to-market strategy is the difference between a product launch that creates momentum and one that lands with zero noise. This module builds a 90-day GTM plan that sequences activities for maximum impact with minimum waste.

---

## 7.1 GTM STRATEGY FOUNDATION

### GTM Motion Selection

The right GTM motion depends on product type and target customer:

| GTM Motion | Description | Best For | Key Metric |
|-----------|-------------|---------|-----------|
| **Product-Led Growth (PLG)** | Product drives acquisition and expansion | SaaS, tools, apps | Free-to-paid conversion |
| **Sales-Led Growth (SLG)** | Sales team drives acquisition and expansion | Enterprise B2B, complex products | Pipeline velocity |
| **Marketing-Led Growth (MLG)** | Content/brand drives acquisition | Consumer, SMB, any with long eval cycle | CAC, MQL volume |
| **Community-Led Growth (CLG)** | Community members become advocates | Developer tools, niche products | Community size, NPS |
| **Partner-Led Growth (PLG)** | Partners distribute the product | Channel-dependent, regional markets | Partner-sourced revenue |

**Hybrid is common:** Start with one motion, add others as the business scales.

### GTM Readiness Checklist

Before executing any launch, verify:

```
PRODUCT
☐ Core features function reliably
☐ Onboarding is functional (new users can get value without help)
☐ Payment processing is set up and tested
☐ Basic documentation exists
☐ Customer support email/channel is live

POSITIONING
☐ Value proposition is written and tested in conversation
☐ Elevator pitch under 30 seconds is rehearsed
☐ Landing page is live with clear value proposition
☐ Pricing is defined (even if provisional)

SALES INFRASTRUCTURE
☐ CRM or lead tracking is set up
☐ Email templates are written
☐ Demo flow is documented and rehearsed
☐ Proposal/quote template exists
☐ Contract or terms of service exists

ANALYTICS
☐ Website analytics is tracking (GA4, Plausible, or similar)
☐ Conversion tracking is set up
☐ Customer data capture is in place
```

## 7.2 PHASE 1 — PRE-LAUNCH (Days 1–14)

**Objective:** Build awareness, validate positioning, recruit early adopters, and de-risk the launch.

### Core Pre-Launch Activities

**A. POSITION VALIDATION**
Run 5–10 conversations with potential customers using this script:
```
Opening: "I'm building [X] for [target]. I'd love 20 minutes to understand if this is relevant to you."
Discovery: "Walk me through how you currently handle [problem]."
Test pitch: "Here's what we do [pitch]. Does this resonate?"
Validation: "If this existed today and worked as described, would you use/pay for it?"
Price test: "If it cost [price], would you sign up? What if it were [higher price]?"
```

**Record EVERY answer. Patterns in responses = validated insights.**

**B. LANDING PAGE CONSTRUCTION**

The minimum viable landing page must include:
- **Hero headline:** Clear benefit, not clever tagline
- **Sub-headline:** Who it's for and what it does
- **3–5 key benefits** (not features — benefits)
- **Social proof:** Even if just "X people are testing this" or a beta customer quote
- **Call to action:** One clear action (sign up / buy / get demo)
- **FAQ:** Address the top 3 objections

**C. WAITLIST / BETA PROGRAM**
Collect early interest. Use a waitlist to:
- Validate demand before spending on paid acquisition
- Build urgency ("Founding member pricing ends at X customers")
- Create feedback loop before full launch
- Generate social proof from early adopters

**D. PARTNERSHIP PRE-WORK**
Identify 3–5 potential distribution partners. Begin relationship before launch.
Partnerships take 30–90 days to materialize — start early.

**E. PRESS / COMMUNITY GROUNDWORK**
Identify:
- 3 relevant industry publications or newsletters
- 5 community moderators or influencers
- 10 potential early adopters who are also content creators

Begin relationships before asking for promotion.

## 7.3 PHASE 2 — LAUNCH (Days 15–30)

**Objective:** Generate initial revenue, acquire first customers, and establish channel presence.

### Launch Week Activities

**DAY 1: THE DROP**
Execute simultaneously:
- Product Hunt launch (if relevant to audience)
- Social media announcement on all active channels
- Email to waitlist
- Community announcement (ask for feedback, not just promotion)
- Partner activation (if pre-arranged)
- LinkedIn post from founder's personal account

**DAY 2–7: SALES SPRINT**
- Direct outreach to 20–50 highest-probability prospects
- Personal email from founder to everyone who signed up for waitlist
- Follow up with everyone who showed interest in pre-launch conversations
- Ask every early user for feedback AND referrals

**DAY 8–30: CHANNEL ESTABLISHMENT**
- Publish first pieces of content on priority channels
- Begin systematic outreach cadence
- Run first small paid advertising test (if economics justify)
- Schedule demos with any interested prospects
- Close first paying customers — this is the ONLY metric that matters this month

## 7.4 PHASE 3 — OPTIMIZATION (Days 31–60)

**Objective:** Understand what is working, double down on it, and cut what is not.

### Conversion Funnel Analysis

Map and measure every step:

```
AWARENESS: How many people know the product exists?
  ↓ [conversion %]
INTEREST: How many visit the landing page?
  ↓ [conversion %]
CONSIDERATION: How many sign up / request a demo?
  ↓ [conversion %]
PURCHASE: How many become paying customers?
  ↓ [conversion %]
RETENTION: How many are still customers after 30 days?
  ↓ [conversion %]
EXPANSION: How many upgrade or buy more?
  ↓ [conversion %]
ADVOCACY: How many refer others?
```

**Find the biggest drop-off. That is where optimization effort goes.**

### Activation Rate Improvement
For product-led products:
- Define "activation" (the moment users first experience core value)
- Measure % of new users who activate within 48 hours
- Identify and remove every obstacle between signup and activation
- Build activation email sequence

### Feedback Collection System
Implement within the first 30 days:
- NPS survey at Day 14 and Day 30 for every customer
- Exit survey for churned customers
- Feature request tracking
- Support ticket analysis (what are they struggling with?)

## 7.5 PHASE 4 — SCALE (Days 61–90)

**Objective:** Identify what is working and systematize it for growth.

### Scale Triggers

Only accelerate a channel or activity when:
1. ✅ CAC is below target threshold
2. ✅ Conversion rates are stable (not declining with volume)
3. ✅ Payback period is acceptable
4. ✅ Retention is positive (customers are staying)

### Scaling Activities

**CHANNEL SCALING**
- Double budget on highest-performing paid channel
- Increase content output on highest-converting organic channel
- Expand outreach volume on best-performing outbound method

**PARTNERSHIP SCALING**
- Activate pre-qualified partners from pre-launch groundwork
- Formalize referral program with clear incentives
- Explore integration partnerships with complementary tools

**INTERNATIONAL CONSIDERATION**
If initial market is showing traction:
- Identify if any existing customers are from other countries
- Assess translation/localization requirements
- Research adjacent market regulations and competitive landscape
