### What Is This?

**NOW WHAT** is a world-class Claude Code skill that activates **THE PRODUCT EMPIRE ARCHITECT** — a 20-persona commercialization intelligence system designed to answer the most critical question every builder faces:

> **I finished building my product. Now what?**

This skill doesn't give you generic advice. It runs a comprehensive intelligence operation across 9 specialized modules to deliver **CEO-grade strategic decisions** about your completed product:

- Who will actually pay for this?

- How should it be priced?
- Which business model wins?
- What are the first 7 actions to take?

### 🛡️ The Anti-AI-Slop Guarantee

This skill enforces the **Anti-AI-Slop Protocol**. It will NEVER:

- ❌ Give generic recommendations
- ❌ Fabricate market data or competitor pricing
- ❌ Promise your product will succeed
- ❌ Recommend paid ads without calculating unit economics
- ❌ List 20 options without picking one
- ❌ Use impressive-sounding terms that lead nowhere
- ❌ Hide uncertainty

It ALWAYS:

- ✅ Makes a decision and defends it
- ✅ Separates facts from assumptions
- ✅ Calculates economic viability before recommending
- ✅ Identifies risks alongside opportunities
- ✅ Prioritizes ruthlessly
- ✅ Tells you what NOT to do
